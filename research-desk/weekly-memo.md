## Focused updates

**MU — FY2026 10-K (filed 10-09).** A memory super-cycle blowout. **Revenue $133.2B, +256% YoY**; **gross margin 80.7% (from 39.8%)**, operating margin 74.6%, **net income $85.0B, diluted EPS $74.33**. DRAM +252% / NAND +274% on ASP ~+180–200%. New segment taxonomy (CMBU/CDBU/MCBU/AEBU); data-center-oriented CMBU+CDBU ≈ 61% of revenue; HBM not quantified in $. Balance sheet swung to **+$38.3B net cash** with ~$59B FCF; inventory up only 24% (tight supply, not a build). **No customer ≥10%** (was 17%), though the data-center *end market* is ~60% of revenue. Key risk changes: a genuinely new **union/strike** risk factor, a new **take-or-pay HBM customer-agreement** risk (and $12.75B of customer deposits repaying FY2029–31), a far-wider **ASP-swing disclosure (+200% / −50%)**, and expanded "**AI demand may not be sustainable**" language. China CAC risk unchanged; only new trade item is a Feb-2026 Section 301 probe. Tone bullish/expansionary (multi-fab buildout, buyback raised to $35.16B).

**AVGO — Q3 FY2026 10-Q (filed 09-10).** **Revenue $29.6B, +86% YoY**; **Semiconductor Solutions +127%** on custom AI accelerators/XPUs + AI networking, Infrastructure Software (VMware VCF) +29% at ~84% margin. GAAP gross margin 69.1%, operating margin 53.9%, **net income $13.1B, EPS $2.68**. ~$32B YTD FCF; **net debt down ~$6B to $35.4B**. No standalone AI-revenue figure, but AI structural dollars are large: **RPO ~$179.2B**, **AI XPV platform** ($35B tranche, >20 GW through 2028) and an off-balance-sheet **~$29B lease backstop**. Risk factors (fully restated) escalated on three fronts: **customer concentration** (one distributor = **50% of Q3 revenue**; top-5 ~55%), a new **AI-"bubble"/market-correction** hedge, and new **XPU/AI-rack vendor-financing** risk. Watch the rev-rec optics: dropping termination-for-convenience pulls software license revenue forward into "products."

**MRVL — Q2 FY2027 10-Q (filed 08-28).** **Revenue $2.74B, +37% YoY**; **Data center = 79% of revenue, +46%**. GAAP gross margin **improved +2.7pp to 53.1%**, and GAAP operating income is **solidly positive ($459.7M, 16.8% margin)** — the "thin GAAP" framing is easing (amortization falling as Inphi/Cavium roll off; SBC the bigger drag now). **EPS $0.33, +58%** for the quarter, but **YTD net income fell −8.1%** on a $433.7M non-cash Celestial earnout remeasurement. Strategic signals: **NVIDIA $2.0B convertible preferred** + custom-XPU partnership, plus a subsequent-event **customer warrant up to 59M shares vesting on ~$12B of revenue** milestones. Red flag: **single-distributor concentration deepening to 44%** of revenue (from 34%). Harsher Item 1A on AI-capex durability, data-center siting, China (NDRC Manus unwind) and TSMC single-source.

## Themes across the watchlist

1. **One driver — AI data center — and it's at super-cycle magnitude.** The three filers inflected on the *same* demand, from different layers of the stack: memory/HBM (MU +256%), custom accelerators + AI networking (AVGO semi +127%), and custom silicon + interconnect (MRVL data center +46%, now 79% of revenue). MU, AVGO and MRVL all now derive the majority (≈60–79%) of revenue from data-center/AI end markets.

2. **Vendor / "circular" financing and customer concentration are escalating together.** AVGO added a ~$29B off-balance-sheet AI-lease backstop and the AI XPV platform; MRVL took a $2.0B NVIDIA preferred and wrote a ~$12B customer warrant; MU collected $12.75B of take-or-pay customer deposits — all echoes of the pattern already flagged in NVDA's note ($279B commitments, $500B financing MOUs). Concentration is the twin: AVGO one distributor at 50% of revenue, MRVL one distributor at 44%. The industry is increasingly funding and locking in its own demand.

3. **Risk-language is turning cautious on AI durability even as the numbers blow out.** AVGO added an explicit AI-"bubble"/market-correction hedge; MU expanded "AI demand may not be sustainable" and widened its ASP-swing disclosure to −50%; MRVL hardened data-center-siting and AI-capex language. Secondary cross-current: trade/China actions keep accreting (MU Section 301 Feb-2026; MRVL NDRC Manus unwind + Section 232 report) without yet being quantified as revenue hits for these three.
